NUTRITION, DIET, AND HEALTHY LIFESTYLE

=== BALANCED DIET FUNDAMENTALS ===

Macronutrients:
- Carbohydrates: Primary energy source. Choose complex carbs (whole grains, legumes, vegetables) over refined sugars and processed foods. Recommended: 45-65% of daily calories.
- Proteins: Essential for tissue repair and immune function. Sources: lean meats, fish, eggs, legumes, nuts, dairy, tofu. Recommended: 10-35% of daily calories.
- Fats: Necessary for hormone production and vitamin absorption. Choose healthy fats (olive oil, avocado, nuts, fatty fish). Limit saturated and trans fats. Recommended: 20-35% of daily calories.

Micronutrients (Key Vitamins and Minerals):
- Vitamin D: Supports bone health and immune function. Sources: sunlight, fortified milk, fatty fish. Deficiency linked to bone disease and weakened immunity.
- Vitamin C: Antioxidant, immune support, collagen synthesis. Sources: citrus fruits, bell peppers, strawberries, broccoli.
- Iron: Oxygen transport, energy metabolism. Sources: red meat, spinach, legumes, fortified cereals. Take with vitamin C to improve absorption.
- Calcium: Bone and teeth health, muscle function. Sources: dairy products, leafy greens, fortified plant milks.
- B12: Nerve function, red blood cell formation. Sources: meat, fish, dairy. Vegans should supplement.
- Folate: Cell division, pregnancy health. Sources: leafy greens, legumes, fortified grains.

Hydration:
- Recommended daily water intake: ~2.7 liters for women, ~3.7 liters for men (including water from food).
- Signs of dehydration: dark yellow urine, fatigue, dizziness, dry mouth, headache.
- Increase intake during exercise, hot weather, illness, or pregnancy.
- Water is the best choice; limit sugary drinks and excessive caffeine.

=== DIETARY PATTERNS FOR HEALTH ===

Mediterranean Diet:
- Emphasizes fruits, vegetables, whole grains, legumes, nuts, olive oil, fish.
- Moderate dairy and poultry; limited red meat and sweets.
- Associated with reduced risk of heart disease, type 2 diabetes, and cognitive decline.

DASH Diet (for blood pressure):
- Reduces sodium intake (less than 2,300 mg/day, ideally 1,500 mg).
- Emphasizes fruits, vegetables, whole grains, lean protein, low-fat dairy.
- Limits saturated fats, sugar, and red meat.

Anti-Inflammatory Diet:
- Focus on colorful fruits and vegetables, omega-3 fatty acids (salmon, walnuts, flaxseed).
- Reduce processed foods, refined carbohydrates, and trans fats.
- Include spices like turmeric and ginger.

Foods to Limit:
- Ultra-processed foods (chips, fast food, packaged snacks)
- Added sugars (sodas, candies, baked goods)
- Refined grains (white bread, white rice)
- High-sodium foods (canned soups, processed meats)
- Trans fats (partially hydrogenated oils)

=== PHYSICAL ACTIVITY ===

Recommended Exercise Guidelines (Adults):
- Aerobic activity: At least 150-300 minutes of moderate intensity per week (e.g., brisk walking, cycling, swimming) OR 75-150 minutes of vigorous intensity.
- Muscle-strengthening: 2 or more days per week targeting all major muscle groups.
- Reduce sedentary time; even light activity is beneficial.

Types of Exercise:
- Cardiovascular/Aerobic: Walking, running, swimming, cycling, dancing. Improves heart health, burns calories, boosts mood.
- Strength Training: Weightlifting, bodyweight exercises (push-ups, squats). Builds muscle mass, increases metabolism, supports bone health.
- Flexibility: Yoga, stretching. Improves range of motion, reduces injury risk.
- Balance: Tai chi, balance board exercises. Especially important for older adults to prevent falls.

Benefits of Regular Physical Activity:
- Reduces risk of heart disease, type 2 diabetes, and certain cancers.
- Helps maintain healthy weight.
- Improves mental health (reduces depression and anxiety).
- Strengthens bones and muscles.
- Improves sleep quality.
- Enhances cognitive function.

=== SLEEP HEALTH ===

Recommended Sleep Duration:
- Adults: 7-9 hours per night.
- Teenagers: 8-10 hours.
- School-age children: 9-12 hours.

Sleep Hygiene Tips:
- Maintain a consistent sleep schedule (same time to bed and wake up, even on weekends).
- Create a relaxing bedtime routine (reading, warm bath, light stretching).
- Keep bedroom cool, dark, and quiet.
- Avoid screens (phone, TV, computer) 1 hour before bed.
- Limit caffeine after 2 PM.
- Avoid large meals and alcohol close to bedtime.
- Regular physical activity improves sleep quality (but not too close to bedtime).

Effects of Poor Sleep:
- Increased risk of obesity, diabetes, cardiovascular disease.
- Weakened immune system.
- Impaired cognitive function, memory, and concentration.
- Increased risk of mood disorders.

=== STRESS MANAGEMENT ===

Healthy Stress Management Techniques:
- Regular physical exercise (natural mood booster via endorphins).
- Mindfulness meditation and deep breathing exercises.
- Progressive muscle relaxation.
- Maintaining social connections with supportive friends and family.
- Time management and setting realistic goals.
- Journaling and expressive writing.
- Hobbies and creative activities.
- Limiting news and social media consumption.
- Adequate sleep.

=== PREVENTIVE HEALTHCARE ===

Regular Health Screenings (Consult your doctor for personalized schedule):
- Blood pressure: Starting at age 18, checked every 1-2 years if normal.
- Cholesterol: Starting at age 20, every 4-6 years if low risk.
- Blood glucose: Starting at age 45, or earlier if overweight/obese.
- Colorectal cancer: Starting at age 45-50.
- Breast cancer: Mammograms starting at age 40-50 (varies by guidelines).
- Cervical cancer: Pap smear starting at age 21.
- Dental checkups: Every 6-12 months.
- Eye exams: Every 1-2 years.

Vaccinations:
- Annual flu vaccine recommended for everyone over 6 months.
- COVID-19 vaccine and boosters per current guidelines.
- Tetanus booster every 10 years.
- Pneumococcal vaccine for adults 65+.
- Shingles vaccine for adults 50+.
- HPV vaccine for preteens, teens, and young adults.

Substance Use:
- Smoking: No safe level. Smoking cessation reduces health risks significantly within weeks to years. Resources available for quitting.
- Alcohol: If consumed, limit to moderate levels (up to 1 drink/day for women, 2 for men). No alcohol is safest for health overall.
